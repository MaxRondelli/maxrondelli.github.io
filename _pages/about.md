I am a **PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Bologna**. My fellowship was funded by the **Luna Rossa Team**, the America's Cup Team.

My research focuses on **Reinforcement Learning**, specifically on how the agents behave and take decisions in competitive scenarios.

Before my PhD, I earned a double M.Sc. degree through a joint program between the University of Bologna (Computer Science) and Bielefeld University (Interactive Intelligent Systems). 
During my Master's, I spent a year working with the Ducati MotoGP Team on a funded research scholarship, building a Computer Vision tool that estimates rider posture on the bike.

**Research interests:** Reinforcement Learning, Multi-Agent Systems, Machine Learning

I am always happy to chat about research and collaborations, so feel free to reach out!
